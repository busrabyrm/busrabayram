My research focuses on wireless communication systems, aerial networks, software-defined radio (SDR), and Integrated Sensing and Communication (ISAC). My current work particularly investigates multi-static ISAC architectures, ISAC-assisted wireless networks, and adaptive communication mechanisms for UAVs.

## Multi-Static ISAC for Low-Altitude Networks

My research investigates multi-static Integrated Sensing and Communication architectures for low-altitude communication and sensing systems.

The work focuses on dynamic resource allocation, role management, and UAV-aware ISAC mechanisms for composite cell architectures, where multiple network nodes can collaboratively perform communication and sensing functions.

**Research topics:**
- Multi-static ISAC architectures
- Low-altitude communication and sensing
- Dynamic resource allocation
- ISAC node role management
- UAV-aware ISAC

## ISAC-Assisted Dynamic Indoor Wi-Fi Coverage

This research investigates the use of ISAC-based environmental awareness to improve dynamic indoor Wi-Fi coverage.

Rather than focusing solely on static access-point placement, the work explores how sensing information can be used for real-time network adaptation and reconfiguration in response to changes in the wireless environment.

**Research topics:**
- ISAC-assisted Wi-Fi
- Environment-aware wireless networks
- Dynamic coverage optimization
- Access-point adaptation
- Real-time network reconfiguration

## Flight-Dynamics-Aware Aerial Communications

This research investigates adaptive communication mechanisms that incorporate UAV flight dynamics into wireless link adaptation.

In particular, flight and motion information obtained from onboard sensors such as IMUs can be used to support adaptive modulation and coding decisions for air-to-ground communication links.

**Research topics:**
- UAV communications
- Air-to-ground (A2G) links
- Adaptive modulation and coding
- Flight-dynamics-aware communication
- IMU-assisted link adaptation
- SDR-based experimental validation
